Social media

For businesses that need to look alive where their customers already spend time.

Starting at$1,200 / month

What you get

  • A monthly content calendar you approve before anything posts

Paid ads

For businesses that want a predictable number of leads or sales and can say what one is worth.

Starting at$1,500 / month

What you get

  • Google Search and Shopping, Meta, and TikTok campaigns

  • New ad creative every month, written and designed by us
  • Budget and bid changes every week, logged with the reason
  • A monthly report that ends in cost per lead or cost per sale

What we need from you

  • Your ad accounts, with us added as an authorized user
  • A monthly ad budget, paid to the platforms directly
  • A straight answer on what a lead or a sale is worth to you

Questions we get on the call

Is there a contract?

Management and ad work is month to month with 30 days' notice. Website builds are a fixed price, paid half at the start and half at launch.

Can you work on my existing site?

Yes. We audit it first and tell you whether to fix it or rebuild it. Sometimes a rebuild is not worth the money.

How much ad spend do I need?

We prefer accounts spending at least $1,500 a month. Below that there is rarely enough data to optimize, and we will say so on the call instead of taking your fee.

Who writes the posts and the ads?

We do, and you approve the monthly calendar before anything goes live. Ad copy goes through the same approval.
